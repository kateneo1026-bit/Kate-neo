# Hook library: hidden stories

There are 30 formulas to reuse. Fill in the blanks [ ] with your own place, and say or show the hook in the **first 2 seconds**.
Tip: put the hook as text on screen **and** say it in the voiceover. Start on movement (a door opening, steam from a coffee, footsteps).

The examples are templates. Check every fact before you post.

## Secrets and hidden places
1. **"Most people walk past [place] without knowing [secret]."**
   *Most people walk past this door in Soho without knowing it's a café.*
2. **"There's a [thing] hidden behind [ordinary thing] in [city]."**
   *There's a garden hidden behind this church wall in London.*
3. **"You'd never find this [place] unless someone told you. So I'm telling you."**
4. **"This [building/street] has a secret it doesn't advertise."**
5. **"The locals in [city] don't put this on Instagram."**
6. **"If you're in [city], go through the door nobody opens."**

## Strange details
7. **"Look closely at [detail]. It's not decoration."**
   *Look closely at this wall. Those marks aren't decoration.*
8. **"Why does this [building] have [odd feature]?"**
   *Why does this house have a window painted on?*
9. **"This [thing] is [surprising number] years old and still [doing something]."**
10. **"Nobody knows why [strange fact], but here's the best theory."**
11. **"The smallest [thing] in [city/country] is hiding here."**
12. **"This street looks normal. Until you look up."**

## Time and history
13. **"[Number] years ago, this [place] was [completely different thing]."**
    *200 years ago, this café was a bank vault.* [check]
14. **"This [place] has served [thing] since [year]. Nothing has changed."**
15. **"Before it was a [current use], it was a [past use]."**
16. **"Someone lived here in [year], and they left something behind."**
17. **"This is what [city] looked like before the tourists came."**

## Quiet and slow moments
18. **"The quietest [time] in [city] happens here."**
    *The quietest morning in Paris happens here, at 7am.*
19. **"I came here for [ordinary thing] and stayed for [unexpected thing]."**
20. **"A quiet [day/morning] in [place], with no crowds, no rush."**
21. **"This is how [city] sounds at [time]."** (put the sound first, words second)
22. **"One [coffee/table/bench] in [city] that feels like a secret."**

## Curiosity and contrast
23. **"Everyone goes to [famous place]. The locals go [here]."**
24. **"[Famous place] is busy. [Hidden place] 5 minutes away is empty."**
25. **"I almost didn't open this door."**
26. **"You need to know this before you visit [city]."**
27. **"Don't go to [city] without seeing [hidden place]."**

## Lifestyle (brown and cosy)
28. **"The [object] that makes my [room] feel like an old [hotel/café]."**
    *The lamp that makes my bedroom feel like an old Paris hotel.*
29. **"A slow [morning/Sunday] in [colour] tones."**
    *A slow Sunday in brown tones.*
30. **"Things in my home that feel like they have a story."**

## Famous places, surprising facts (best reach so far)
31. **"In [year], [famous place] [event]. [Something unexpected] survived / stayed."**
    *In 2019, Notre-Dame burned. 180,000 bees on its roof survived.* [check]
32. **"Everyone knows [famous building]. Almost nobody knows [impossible origin]."**
    *Everyone knows Liberty. Almost nobody knows it's made of warships.*
33. **"[Famous building] was never supposed to [last / be here / look like this]."**

**Send test:** before using any hook, ask "Would someone send this to a friend with 'look at this'?" If not, find a stronger twist. (24 Nov: 8 of 9 reels stopped at ~120 views with 0 sends; the one with a send reached 431.)

---
**Make your own:** strange detail + specific place + a reason to keep watching. Keep it to 12 words or fewer.
