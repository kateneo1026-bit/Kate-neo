# Weekly review (every Tuesday, before writing next week's plan)

Run this **before** the next weekly plan. Its decisions go into section 1 ("What changed from last week") of the new plan.
Save it as `content-plan/reviews/week-YYYY-MM-DD-review.md` (date = the Wednesday of the week being reviewed).

---

## 1. Collect the numbers
From the daily log (`content-plan/reviews/week-…-log.md`), Instagram Insights, Metricool (if connected), ManyChat and the affiliate dashboards.

| Post | Type (ambient / story / carousel) | Views | Avg watch | Shares | Saves | Follows | Keyword comments | Link clicks | Affiliate £ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Account level: followers start → end · Story views/day · Story replies · Broadcast Channel members · total link clicks · total affiliate earnings.

## 2. Calculate the rates (per 1,000 views, so small and big posts compare fairly)
- **Share rate** = shares ÷ views × 1000 → the best signal of reach on Instagram.
- **Save rate** = saves ÷ views × 1000 → value / guide quality.
- **Follow rate** = follows ÷ views × 1000 → the account promise works.
- **Click rate** = link clicks ÷ keyword comments → the DM and offer work.
- **Hold** = avg watch ÷ reel length → the hook and motion work.

## 3. Diagnose (pick the fix)
| What you see | Likely cause | Fix next week |
|---|---|---|
| Low hold (< 50%) | Weak first frame / hook | Stronger strange hook; start on the moving element; shorter reel |
| Good views, low follows | No promise / no series | Series name on screen, "follow for Part 2", pin it |
| High saves, low clicks | Link too far away | Keyword DM + Story link the same day; numbered bio link |
| High shares, low saves | Mood is good, value is low | Add one practical line in the caption (where / price / how) |
| Low everything on an AI clip | Looked fake | Check the clip against the Cinematic Generation Standard v3 and the Kate identity block; simplify the scene |
| Ambient loop beats story reels | Audience wants mood | Use 2 ambient loops next week, keep the affiliate link in caption and Stories |

## 4. Decide (max 3 changes a week)
- **Keep** the 2 best formats/topics.
- **Drop** the worst one.
- **Test** 1 new thing (a new hook type, a new set, a new text line, a new posting time).

## 5. Update the skill files
- `flow-prompt-lessons.md`: lessons from the daily logs. A lesson that worked **2 times** → move it to **Top rules**. One that failed twice → negative list.
- `hooks-ctas-hashtags.md`: add the best hook and best keyword of the week. Note hashtags that brought reach.
- `brand-kate.md`: only if Kate's look/sets changed.
- `audience-psychology.md`: add any new pain or desire seen in comments/DMs (use people's real words).
