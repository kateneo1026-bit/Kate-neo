# Audience, needs, pain and psychology (UK + Europe)

## Who we are talking to
- **Core:** women 22–38 in the UK (London, Manchester, Leeds, Bristol, Edinburgh) and big European cities
  (Dublin, Amsterdam, Paris, Berlin, Lisbon, Madrid, Milan). Often renting, working full-time, saving money, a bit tired of loud content.
- **Secondary:** couples planning city breaks; gift buyers (Nov–Dec); people who just moved into their first flat.
- **Where they scroll:** in bed before sleep, on the commute, at lunch, Sunday evening. That's why posting times are 07:30, 12:30 and 20:30 UK time.

## What people need (the desire)
1. **A calm escape.** Five quiet minutes away from news, work and noise. Brown, warm and slow feels safe.
2. **To feel "put together" on a budget.** A home that looks expensive, a life with rituals, without a big salary.
3. **Real places, not tourist traps.** Travel that feels like a discovery and makes them feel clever.
4. **Fewer decisions.** "Just tell me where to stay / eat / what lamp to buy." Ready lists save time.
5. **A small identity.** "I'm the kind of person who likes quiet, beautiful, hidden things."
6. **Company without pressure.** Faceless + voice = the feeling of a friend, without comparing themselves to a perfect face.

## Psychology levers (how each post converts)
- **Curiosity gap (hidden stories):** open a question in the first 2 seconds and only close it near the end, so people watch to the end and the algorithm pushes the reel.
- **Specificity = believability:** "a café with 1,200 books behind the counter" is better than "a cute café".
- **Pattern interrupt:** strange first frame or claim ("This hotel has no reception").
- **Open loops and series:** "Hidden Story No. 7", "Part 2 tomorrow", which makes people follow so they don't miss the next one.
- **Utility → saves:** lists, maps, prices and routes are saved. Saves tell Instagram the content has value.
- **Shareability:** "Send this to the person you'd go with" works for travel because the decision is shared.
- **Reciprocity:** give a free useful thing (a guide or list in DMs) and people trust the link.
- **Identity and belonging:** call the audience "the quiet ones". People follow identities, not accounts.
- **Seasonal scarcity / timing:** "Opens this weekend", "Only in December", "Prices rise after Friday" (only if true).
- **Low-friction CTA:** commenting one word feels small, the auto-DM delivers the link, and that comment also boosts reach.
- **Soft authority:** small facts, dates and history make Kate feel like the friend who knows.

## The ladder: viewer → follower → tribe → buyer
| Stage | Goal | Content that does it | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Viewer (discover)** | Stop the scroll | Strange-hook reels, hidden places, seasonal stories | Reach, 3-sec hold, watch time |
| **Follower** | "I want more of this" | Series, consistent look, promise in bio, "follow for Part 2" | Follows per reel, profile visits |
| **Tribe** | "This is my place" | Stories every day, polls, Broadcast Channel, Sunday Reset ritual, replying to DMs, naming them "the quiet ones" | Story replies, DMs, shares, returning viewers |
| **Buyer** | Click and book/buy | Keyword DMs, Story link stickers, numbered link-in-bio, guides | Link clicks, affiliate conversions |

**Weekly mix (fixed format: 4 Reels + 1 Carousel):** 2 discover Reels, 2 follow/tribe Reels, 1 buy Carousel. Every post still has a CTA, and Stories add a link sticker every day.

## Daily Story rhythm (3 per day)
1. **Morning (07:30): mood.** A calm clip or still (coffee, window, rain), with a poll or "Quiet Question".
2. **Midday (12:30): value + link.** Behind the post / extra tip, plus a link sticker (affiliate, labelled #ad).
3. **Evening (20:30): tribe.** Question box, "this or that", results of the poll, a reply to a DM (shown anonymously), or a teaser of tomorrow.

## Learning loop (every week)
Ask the user for, or pull, per post: views, avg watch time, saves, shares, follows, comments on the keyword, link clicks.
- High views but few follows → the hook works but there's no promise. Add series and a "follow for" line.
- High saves but low clicks → move the link closer (keyword DM + Story sticker the same day).
- Low 3-sec hold → rewrite the hook with stronger tension; change the first frame.
- Keep the 2 best formats, test 1 new format each week, and drop the worst.
