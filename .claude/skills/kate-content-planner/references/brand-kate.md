# Kate: brand bible

## Who Kate is
- **Age:** 26. **Lives:** a small rented flat in London (default; change here if the user decides otherwise).
- **Personality:** quiet, observant, curious, a little nostalgic. She notices what others walk past.
  Calm, never loud. She doesn't show off; she *lets you in on something*.
- **Tagline idea:** *"Quiet routines & hidden stories."*
- **Faceless:** the viewer *is* Kate (POV) or sees her from behind, her hands, her silhouette.
- **AI character:** her bio says so openly (see compliance file). This builds trust; it doesn't hurt reach.

### Recognisable "Kate signatures" (keep identical in every Flow prompt)
- Hands: slim, **almond nails painted mocha brown**, a **thin gold ring on the right index finger**, a small gold watch with a brown leather strap.
- Hair (back view only): **long chestnut-brown hair in a low claw-clip bun**.
- Clothes: **oversized camel knit jumper**, cream linen shirt, chocolate wool coat, ribbed brown socks.
- Objects: a **speckled stoneware mug in caramel glaze**, a **worn brown leather notebook**, a brass candle holder.
- Flat: walnut wood, linen curtains, cream bouclé chair, warm lamps, a small balcony with plants.

## Brown vibes: visual system
- **Palette:** espresso #3B2A20 · mocha #6F4E37 · caramel #C68E54 · latte #D9B99B · cream #F3E9DC · terracotta accent #B5653A · a little olive green #6B6B47.
- **Light:** golden hour, warm tungsten lamps (2700K), candlelight, soft window light, rainy grey outside / warm inside.
- **Texture:** linen, wool, wood grain, steam, paper, ceramics, film grain.
- **Prompt suffix (paste at the end of every Flow prompt):**
  `warm brown and cream colour palette, soft golden light, 35mm film look, shallow depth of field, gentle film grain, cosy and calm mood, no faces visible, vertical 9:16`
- **Text on screen:** one serif font (e.g. *Playfair Display* or Instagram's "Classic"), cream text,
  never more than 7 words on screen at once.
- **Covers:** still frame + 3–5 words in the same serif font, so the grid looks like one brown story.
- **Sound:** soft lo-fi piano / ambient + natural sound (rain, kettle, pages, footsteps, cutlery).
  No loud trending audio.

## Voice (writing and ElevenLabs)
- First person, short sentences, a gentle British voice. Sounds like a secret told at night.
- Uses: "Nobody tells you…", "I keep coming back to…", "Here's the part most people miss."
- Avoids: shouting, "OMG", "you NEED this", too many emojis (max 3 per caption).

**Weekly balance (4 Reels + 1 Carousel):** default = Reel for routines (1), Reel for hidden places (2),
Reel for hidden tables (3), Reel for helpful home (4), Carousel for a guide (5). Shift it based on what converts,
but keep at least 1 travel and 1 home Reel every week.

## Recurring series (create habit and anticipation)
- **"Hidden Story No. X"**: the story behind a place, object or tradition. Number them, because numbers make people want to collect them all.
- **"Quiet Mornings"**: routine reels, same opening shot every time (kettle + window).
- **"The Brown Edit"**: home and decor finds, once a week.
- **"Kate's Sunday Reset"**: Sunday evening, calm, practical.
- **"Hidden Tables"**: one restaurant or food tradition a week.

## Tribe identity
- Default tribe name: **"the quiet ones"** (people who like slow, warm, hidden things). Change it if the user picks another.
- Rituals: Sunday Reset every week, a "Hidden List" in the Broadcast Channel every Friday, "Quiet Question" Story poll every day.
