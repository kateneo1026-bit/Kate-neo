## Disclosure rules (do this on every affiliate post)
- **UK (ASA / CMA):** affiliate content is advertising. Put **"#ad"** or **"Ad · affiliate links"** at the **start** of the caption, visible before "more".
  Stories with affiliate link stickers need **"#ad"** on the frame.
- **EU:** similar rules (e.g. France's influencer law requires clear "publicité"). Keep the label at the start and in English.
- **Amazon:** "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases" in the bio or link page.

## AI transparency
- **Bio line:** "AI-made world of Kate ✦ quiet routines & hidden stories".
- Turn on Instagram's **AI label** on photorealistic AI video/images.
- **No fake experiences or results.** Kate is fictional, so she must not claim she bought, used, saved or stayed somewhere as real proof
  (that would be a misleading testimonial in an ad). Instead:
  - ✅ "This is how the free-cancellation trick works."
  - ✅ "The lamp that turns a grey flat golden."
  - ✅ "Hidden Story: why Lyon's bouchons…"
  - ❌ "I saved £140 with this." ❌ "I've used this for two years."
- Prices: write "from £X" and check them on the day. Event dates: check them every year.
- Don't show real people's faces in generated content, don't copy real brands' logos, and don't make fake reviews.
