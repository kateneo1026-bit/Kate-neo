# Production: Google Flow (free) + ElevenLabs

> Tool limits change often. Check Google's Flow page and ElevenLabs pricing page every month and update this file.
> Figures below were collected in Nov 2026 from third-party reviews, not official pages.

## Google Flow, free tier: what it means for planning
- Around **50 credits per day** (they do not roll over). **Veo 3.1 Lite ≈ 10 credits per clip** (~8 seconds),
  so about **5 video clips a day = ~35 clips a week**. Veo 3.1 Fast ≈ 20 and Quality ≈ 100 credits are too costly for the free tier.
- Generation may be slower or blocked at peak hours. Generate early morning UK time.
- No upscaling on free, and every output has an invisible **SynthID** watermark (it's an AI-made file and Instagram may detect it).
- **Image generation in Flow** is useful for carousels, covers, Stories and the reference "ingredients". Check its current credit cost in the app.

### Weekly credit budget (default plan)
| Item | Clips | Credits (Lite) |
|---|---|---|
| 5 Reels × 4 clips | 20 | 200 |
| Re-dos (things go wrong) | 6 | 60 |
| Story clips (reuse leftovers + stills) | 7 | 70 |
| **Total** | **33** | **~330 of ~350** |

**Daily batching:** 5 clips per day. Generate tomorrow's reel today. Carousels and most Stories use **images** and reused reel frames.

### Stretch the credits
- One 8-second clip → **2–3 shots**: cut it, crop it, slow it down (speed 0.8×), reverse it.
- Reuse "B-roll" clips (kettle, rain window, candle, hands turning pages) across many reels and Stories.
- Use still images + Instagram's zoom/pan for slow "Ken Burns" shots.
- Write prompts carefully the first time (below), because re-dos waste credits.

## Character consistency (faceless Kate)
1. Create **3 reference images** once (Flow image generation) and save them as "ingredients":
   - `kate-hands`: hands holding the caramel mug, mocha nails, gold ring, camel knit sleeves.
   - `kate-back`: back view, chestnut hair in a claw-clip bun, chocolate wool coat.
   - `kate-flat`: living room with walnut wood, linen curtains, cream bouclé chair, warm lamps.
2. Use **Ingredients to Video** with the right reference for each clip.
3. Always include "no faces visible" and the brand suffix.

## Flow prompt formula
`[Shot type + camera move], [what Kate's hands/back are doing], [place + objects], [light + time], [sound cue], + BRAND SUFFIX`

Example:
`Close-up slow push-in, a woman's hands with mocha nails and a thin gold ring pour hot coffee into a caramel stoneware mug, wooden kitchen counter by a rainy window, early morning blue light mixing with warm lamp light, steam rising, soft rain sound, warm brown and cream colour palette, soft golden light, 35mm film look, shallow depth of field, gentle film grain, cosy and calm mood, no faces visible, vertical 9:16`

**Shot types that work for faceless:** POV hands, over-the-shoulder, back view walking, top-down (flat lay),
feet/shoes on cobbles, reflection in a window (blurred), silhouette against a window, slow pan of a room or street.

**Avoid in prompts:** text or signs (AI letters come out wrong, so add text in Instagram instead), crowds' faces in close-up,
real brand logos, famous landmarks shown wrong. For a real place, use generic but believable details
(timbered houses, stone alley, tram). Show it as "inspired by" and use real names only in the voiceover and caption.

## ElevenLabs
### ⚠️ Licence check
The **free plan is non-commercial and needs attribution**. Affiliate content is commercial.
To use Kate's voice in affiliate reels, use the **Starter plan (about $5–6/month)** or higher, which includes a commercial licence.
Until then: make reels with **text on screen + music only**, or use the voice only in non-affiliate posts and add the credit "Voice: ElevenLabs".

### Kate's voice
- Pick from the Voice Library or use **Voice Design** with:
  *"Young British woman, mid-twenties, soft and warm, calm, slightly breathy, intimate, like telling a secret late at night, neutral Southern English accent."*
- Save it as **"Kate"** and always use the same voice.
- Suggested settings: **Stability 45–55 · Similarity 75 · Style 10–20 · Speed 0.90–0.95**. Use the newest multilingual model.
- Text tricks: `…` for a pause, short sentences, write numbers as words ("twenty-five minutes"), put stress words on their own line.
- Length: **~2.3 words per second**, so a 25-second reel needs ~55–60 words.
- Credits: free ≈ 10,000 characters/month (≈ 10 minutes). One week of VO ≈ 5 × 350 chars = ~1,750 chars.

## Edit in Instagram (free)
1. Upload the clips in order and trim each to 2–4 seconds.
2. Add the ElevenLabs audio, plus quiet music at 10–15% volume.
3. Add serif text in cream: hook in the first frame, then small captions (or auto-captions).
4. Cover: a still frame + 3–5 words.
5. Turn on the **AI label** ("AI info") before posting.
