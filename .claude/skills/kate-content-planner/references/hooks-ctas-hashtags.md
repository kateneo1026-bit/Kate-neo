# Hooks, CTAs, captions, hashtags

## Strange hook types (≤ 12 words, on screen + first VO line)
| Type | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Impossible fact | "This [place] has/does [odd thing]." | "This town hall becomes an advent calendar every December." |
| Forbidden | "[Thing] is illegal/forbidden until [moment]." | "This wine can't be sold until tonight at midnight." |
| Contrarian | "Everyone does X. The locals do Y." | "Everyone books Copenhagen. Go 35 minutes away instead." |
| Secret ritual | "I do [odd thing] every [time]. Here's why." | "I switch off the big light at 4pm. On purpose." |
| Hidden history | "Nobody tells you why [ordinary thing]…" | "Nobody tells you why British puddings are stirred today." |
| Tiny number | "[Small number] [things] that [big result]." | "Three £10 things that make a rented flat feel warm." |
| Warning | "Don't [common action] before you know this." | "Don't book a Christmas market trip before watching this." |
| POV | "POV: you finally found the [hidden place]." | "POV: the café behind the bookshop door." |

**Tests:** Is it specific? Is it a little odd? Does it open a question that only the end of the reel answers?
First frame must *show* the strangeness (a lit window, a knock on a door, a candle at 4pm).

## CTA bank (one per post, rotate)
- **Comment keyword (main):** "Comment **WORD** and I'll send you [the list / the route / the hotels]."
- **Save:** "Save this for [December / your next rainy Sunday]."
- **Share:** "Send this to the person you'd go with."
- **Follow (open loop):** "Hidden Story No. 4 tomorrow. Follow so you don't miss it."
- **Bio number:** "It's number 3 in my bio."
- **Tribe:** "Tell me your one small ritual; I'm collecting them for The Quiet Club."
Keyword rules: one short CAPITAL word, linked to the post (CAVE, LYON, WARM). Never reuse it in the same month.

## Caption structure
1. `Ad · affiliate links` (when there are links)
2. Line 1 = the hook again, in other words (it shows before "more").
3. 2–4 short lines: the hidden story + practical info (📍 where · 🚆 how · 🛏️ stay · £ price from).
4. CTA (keyword) + a second soft CTA (save/share).
5. A tribe line, sometimes: "for The Quiet Club ✦"
6. 5 hashtags.
Max 3 emojis. Short lines. Use keywords people search for (Instagram search reads captions): e.g. "cosy bedroom ideas", "Lyon restaurants".
