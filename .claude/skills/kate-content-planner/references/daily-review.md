# Daily review (10 minutes a day)

Use it when the user says "daily review", "check today's clip", or sends today's numbers or new screenshots.
Save each day's notes in `content-plan/reviews/week-YYYY-MM-DD-log.md` (date = that week's Wednesday), one block per day.

---

## 1. Before generating (2 min)
- [ ] Open today's post in the weekly plan. Is it an **ambient loop** or a **hidden-story reel**?
- [ ] Read the **Top rules** in `flow-prompt-lessons.md`.
- [ ] Check the prompt follows **structure v3** (`cinematic-generation-standard.md`): SCENE · KATE identity block · CAMERA (position, lens, DoF, one move) · LIGHT (motivated) · MATERIALS · ENVIRONMENT · MOOD & COLOUR · QUALITY · SOUND · AVOID.
- [ ] Composition: body trace · 1–2 hero objects · window/layers · warm-inside/cool-outside · ONE gentle moving element · plain unlabelled objects.
- [ ] Same identity, outfit, props and light direction as the other clips of this reel (continuity note).
- [ ] Remove anything on the negative list.
- [ ] Credits left today: ___ / 50. Use Veo 3.1 Lite (≈10 credits).

## 2. After generating: score each clip (3 min)
Score 0–5 for each:
| Criterion | 5 = perfect | Automatic redo if… |
|---|---|---|
| Realism | Photographic realism, natural imperfections, no plastic textures | plastic skin, melting objects, floating cups |
| Palette | Brown/caramel/cream + warm vs cool contrast | orange cast, grey and flat, neon |
| Faceless | No faces at all | **any face** of Kate or a clear face in the background |
| Kate identity | Fair skin with warm undertones, mocha nails, gold ring, oatmeal cuff / chestnut claw-clip hair | **wrong skin tone**, missing nails/ring, different outfit from the other clips |
| Hands & body | Five natural fingers, believable grip and weight, natural movement | 6 fingers, merged/blurred fingers, twisted wrist, morphing |
| Lighting & camera | Motivated light, controlled highlights, one smooth camera move | glow, flicker, sudden exposure change, jerky move |
| Text & logos | None (plain labels) | letters, labels or brand logos appear |
| Motion & mood | One calm movement (rain, steam, flame) | jerky camera, things jump |

- **Keep** if the total is **≥ 32/40** and there is no automatic redo.
- **Redo** by changing **one thing only** in the prompt (e.g. add "hands partly out of frame", remove an object). Write down what changed.
- **Rescue without credits:** crop, slow to 0.8×, cut the bad second, add a light warm filter in Instagram.

- If the user sends inspiration screenshots, analyse each one: format, palette, composition, moving element, text line, sound, numbers on screen → add the lessons to the log.
