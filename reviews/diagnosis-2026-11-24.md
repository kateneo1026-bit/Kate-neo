## The short answer

## The 5 trigger points (most important first)

### 1. No "send trigger" (the main one)
The warships reel got its share because the fact is something you **tell a friend** ("did you know Liberty is made of warships?"). The parrot is charming but nobody needs to send it.
**Fix:** every hidden story must pass the test *"Would someone send this to a friend with 'look at this'?"*. Pick stories with a twist (a survivor, an impossible origin, a reversal), and say it out loud at the end: **"Send this to someone who loves Paris."**

### 2. Your followers are not real fans (yet)
- Monday's Stories: **2 viewers** out of 15 followers, plus hidden "spam" viewers. The poll got **0 votes**.
- Reels: **0%** of views from followers.
- 68 following / 15 followers suggests most followers are follow-backs, not readers.

So each reel starts cold, with no friends to give it the first like, comment or send.
**Fix:** don't follow-for-follow. Keep the daily comment routine (5 real comments in your niche). Ask 3–5 real friends to watch and comment early in the first hour. Reply to every comment.

### 3. The profile doesn't give a reason to follow
- The bio is still *"Quiet days and hidden stories ☕🤎"*. The plan's new bio (the series promise) was not changed on Monday.
- The name field is only "kate", so search can't find you.
- No highlights, and the grid mixes history reels with a photo collage.
**Fix (5 minutes, today):**
- **Name:** `Kate · Hidden London & Europe`
- **Bio:** `Hidden stories behind famous places 🗝️ / London & Europe · new story Mon, Wed, Fri ☕🤎`
- Pin your 3 best reels: warships, king's door, parrot.

### 4. The audience is not who the plan thinks
The plan targets *women 25–45 who like cosy homes*. The real viewers of the parrot reel:
**70% men · 50% aged 45+ · 71% UK** (then Australia, Ireland, Germany, US).
These are **history and London lovers**. They come for the story, not the lamp. Lifestyle posts (collage 2s watch, Christmas market 88 views) confuse Instagram about who to show you to.
**Fix:** make **hidden stories the main lane** (at least 2 of 3 reels). Keep the brown, cosy look as the *style* of every reel, not as a separate topic.

### 5. Small things that add up
- **Hook text over bright lamps** (parrot): the words sit on the two light bulbs and are hard to read. ~30% left by 3s. Put the text on a dark, calm area (top third).
- **Typo in Monday's poll:** "Cut" instead of "Cat". Check sticker spelling before posting.
- **VPN on while using Instagram.** Logging in from changing countries can look like suspicious activity and may hurt trust. Turn the VPN off when you post and engage. (Not proven to cause the wall, but easy to remove.)
- **Gaps:** nothing posted 20–22 Nov. Regular posting keeps the test group warm.
