## 1. The Kate formula (use it on every post)

Every piece of content follows the same pattern. People should feel they've been told a secret:

1. **The strange hook (0–2s):** an odd, specific claim that makes people stop scrolling ("This town hall is a giant advent calendar.")
2. **The quiet reveal (2–20s):** slow, cinematic shots, calm voiceover or text on screen. Don't rush it.
3. **The useful bit (20–30s):** how to get there, where to stay, what it costs. This is where the affiliate link comes in.
4. **The soft CTA:** "Comment **WORD** and I'll send you the link" (use an auto-DM tool such as ManyChat) or "Link in my Stories today".

**Visual style:** warm grade, natural sound (rain, footsteps, church bells, cutlery), serif text overlay, no loud trending audio. Use quiet piano or ambient audio at low volume under the voiceover.
**Reel length:** 15–35 seconds. **Cover:** a still frame plus 3–5 words of text in the same font every time, so the grid looks consistent.
