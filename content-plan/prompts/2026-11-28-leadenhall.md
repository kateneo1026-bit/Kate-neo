**Facts for the voiceover (checked):** the basilica and forum was the largest Roman complex north of the Alps (Look Up London, On London). The pier was found when the market was built, 1880–82. Staff usually show visitors the pier if they ask. Dates vary by source (first built around AD 70, enlarged AD 90–120), so the voiceover says no year.
