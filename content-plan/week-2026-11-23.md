**Hook (formula 1), slide 1:** *"Most people walk past these 6 things in London without noticing."*

**Slides (8):**
1. Cover: a close-up of a strange detail + the title
2. **London Stone** (Cannon Street): an ancient stone behind a grille. Nobody knows its true origin.
3. **Postman's Park:** a wall of tiles for ordinary people who died saving others (the Watts Memorial)
4. **Cleopatra's Needle:** look at the base. The marks are damage from a 1917 air raid. [check]
5. **Philpot Lane:** two tiny mice eating cheese, carved high on a building wall. Said to be London's smallest statue [check]
6. **Seven Noses of Soho:** plaster noses hidden on walls. Can you find one? [check how many are left]
7. **Goodwin's Court:** a tiny alley with gas lamps and old bow windows, near Covent Garden
