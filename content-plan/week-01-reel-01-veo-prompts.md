5 clips × ~7s = ~35s · vertical 9:16 · faceless, no host on camera

**Before you generate**
- In Flow, set the aspect ratio to **9:16 (portrait)** in the settings. Writing it in the prompt alone isn't enough.
- Veo makes 8s clips. Trim each to ~7s in CapCut.
- **Voiceover:** record it yourself (or use one TTS voice) as a single track and lay it over the clips. If Veo speaks the lines, you'll get a different voice in every clip. The prompts below ask Veo for **ambient sound only, no speech, no music**.
- Veo can't show the real Valkenburg caves, so these are atmospheric recreations. If you can get licensed footage of the real caves (Gemeentegrot / Fluweelengrot) from stock or the tourist board, use it for clips 2–3 instead.
- Check the 2026 market dates and cave tickets before posting.
