## Today's reel · Hidden London · No. 2: St Dunstan-in-the-East

**Post:** 19:30 UK · ~22s · 4 clips × ~5s · **Location tag:** St Dunstan in the East
**Cover:** "Hidden London · No. 2" + "The church where trees grow inside"

**Facts to check before posting:**
- A medieval church, damaged in the Great Fire of 1666 and repaired ✅
- Christopher Wren added the tower and steeple, 1695–1701 ✅
- Badly damaged in the Blitz in 1941. Only Wren's tower and steeple and the north and south walls survived ✅ (several sources; not "one bomb", so the voiceover says "the Blitz")
- Not rebuilt after the war; the City of London bought the ruins in 1967 and the garden opened on 21 June 1971 ✅ ("let a garden grow" changed to "turned the ruins into a garden": it was planted, not wild)
- Free, about 400 m (~5 min walk) from the Tower of London ✅ · Open 8am–7pm or dusk if earlier (in winter, about 4pm) ✅
- Lawn, trees and a low fountain in the middle of the old nave ✅ (matches the Flow prompts)
