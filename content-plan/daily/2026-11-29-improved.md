## Tomorrow's reel · Hidden Europe · No. 2: the gold church of Porto

**Post:** Sun 29 · **10:00 UK** · ~21s · 4 clips + 1 spare · **Location tag:** Igreja de São Francisco, Porto
**Cover:** "Hidden Europe · No. 2" + "The monks' gold church"
**AI label:** on. Add "Scenes reimagined with AI" to the caption.

⚠️ **Never** use the clips from @aakritiiacharya's reel, not even as a Flow ingredient (copyright). I used her screenshots only to describe the real interior.

**Facts to check before posting:**
- Franciscan friars took a vow of poverty (true).
- The church is Gothic (14th–15th c.); the gilded carved wood ("talha dourada") was added in the 17th–18th c. [check]
- People often say 300–400 kg of gold leaf were used. Numbers vary, so the script says "covered in gold" only [check before adding a number].
- In 1832, during the Siege of Porto, a fire destroyed the convent next to the church (true) [check date].
- The Palácio da Bolsa (Porto's Stock Exchange) was built on the convent's ruins from 1842 (true) [check].
- Today the church is a museum, not a working church; there are catacombs with bones under the floor [check].
