## Facts to check before posting
- St Pancras opened in 1868 for the Midland Railway. Engineer: William Henry Barlow. The hotel in front (Midland Grand Hotel) is by George Gilbert Scott (true).
- The basement (undercroft) was built to store **beer barrels from Burton upon Trent**, and the iron columns were spaced to fit the barrels [check the exact wording].
- Barlow's train shed was the **largest single-span roof in the world** when it was built (about 73 m wide) [check].
- In the 1960s the station was threatened with demolition. A campaign that included the poet **John Betjeman** saved it; it was listed Grade I in 1967. His statue stands on the upper concourse [check].
- Since 2007 Eurostar leaves from St Pancras, and the old beer basement is now the station's main hall with the shops and Eurostar departures [check].
