## Facts to check before posting
- Leadenhall Market's painted iron-and-glass roof was designed by Sir Horace Jones in 1881 (true).
- In *Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone* (2001), the market was used for the Diagon Alley scenes, and a blue door in Bull's Head Passage was the entrance to the Leaky Cauldron (true).
- The market stands on the site of Roman London's basilica and forum (around AD 100), said to be the largest Roman building north of the Alps [check].
- A piece of the Roman basilica wall can be seen in the basement of a barber's shop at the market [check: is it still open and visible in 2026?]. **If not:** change the hook to *"London's real Diagon Alley was built on top of a Roman forum."* and drop clip 3's barber chair.
- Free · nearest stations Bank and Monument · the roofed lanes are open all the time, the shops mostly on weekdays [check].
