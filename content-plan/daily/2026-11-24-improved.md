## Today's reel · Hidden Europe · No. 1: the bees of Notre-Dame

**Post:** 19:30 UK · ~20s · 4 clips × ~5s · **Location tag:** Notre-Dame de Paris
**Cover:** "Hidden Europe · No. 1" + "The bees that survived Notre-Dame"

**Facts to check before posting:**
- Three beehives have sat on the roof of the sacristy, next to the cathedral, since 2013 [check].
- About 180,000 bees lived there (around 60,000 per hive) [check].
- In the fire of 15 April 2019, the hives were about 30 m below the burning roof, and the bees survived [check].
- The beekeeper's explanation: smoke makes bees drowsy, so they stay in the hive and gorge on honey; the wax would only melt at about 63 °C [check].
- Notre-Dame reopened on 7 December 2024 (true).
